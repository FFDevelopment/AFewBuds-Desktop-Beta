AFewBuds Desktop 0.16.0-beta.8

Next desktop beta, built from pinned AFewBuds 3D prototype source e58b3d3ee3114361d0dd103d1a5e993a06a632a4.
Validation: https://github.com/FFDevelopment/afewbuds-3d-prototype/actions/runs/37898579231

Changes since desktop beta.7:
- Add Agent Reeves to persistent Contacts and Messages. Messages support heat status, paid favors, payoff and quiet-exit interactions; keep the Heat phone page focused on monitoring and pressure.
- Preserve previously earned progression and rewards when inventory is consumed, sold or moved; career achievements are historical rather than recalculated solely from current stock.
- Preserve the highest historical number of simultaneously held seed varieties across the apartment and house shelves for milestone rewards.
- Award cumulative stored-grams progression for products put into either owned property's storage without combining their inventories.
- Retain the complete desktop beta.7 house/apartment separation, property-specific computers, workers, inventories, grow tents and ventilation, power usage, furniture previews and walk-around editing, original packaging minigames, couch seating and normal idle animations at work stations.
- Retain desktop mouse and controller inputs, account session, cloud-save career, launcher updates, chapters and the full gameplay baseline.

This release is based on a pinned source commit and requires full desktop regression and exported-runtime validation before publication. Use AFewBuds-Launcher.exe for Windows updates or the complete Windows/Linux ZIP. Existing accounts, saves, purchased property, stock and crew are not intentionally reset.
