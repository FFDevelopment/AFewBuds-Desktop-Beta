AFewBuds Desktop 0.16.0-beta.7

Built from the fully featured AFewBuds 3D prototype, retaining the inventory, cloud account and save synchronization, story progression, free-movement furniture placement and detailed furniture models.

- House and apartment computers now show property-local employee assignments, stored product, production inventory, equipment and utilities instead of mirroring the apartment operation.
- Workers can be reassigned to owned properties; stock, purchases and furniture stay with their original property until moved intentionally.
- House grow room control panel tracks installed grow tents, plants, lighting and water/health alerts and updates when equipment is placed, picked up or plants change.
- House ventilation is a separate installable system: the panel detects the installed unit, shows its operating state and controls it independently of apartment ventilation.
- Apartment and house power usage, lighting, ventilation and crop growth respond to the correct property's installed equipment.
- Kobi, Malik and Rod stop walking and stand normally at packing, growing and storage stations, and use restored seated poses on the saved couch location.
- Original scissors and drag-to-bag minigames are retained with upgraded Bench II/III throughput; buds and scale weights display on placed benches.
- Free-cursor desktop and controller layout controls, actual 3D furniture previews, wall-mounted hidden stashes and closer wall placement are retained.
- Orphaned apartment equipment labels are hidden while the permanent room signage remains.

Validation: publish only after the pinned source commit's full desktop regression suite succeeds. This prerelease uses the existing updater and career-save conventions.
